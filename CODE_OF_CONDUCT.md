# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Argue about the code, not the person.

## What that means here

This project is about measurement, so the standard is a technical one:

- **Bring evidence.** "This number looks wrong" is a fine start; "this number is wrong,
  here is the spec and what I expected" is a contribution.
- **Being right is not a licence.** A correct criticism delivered with contempt still
  costs the project more than it gains.
- **Assume competence.** People here are working on options pricing, market
  microstructure and statistics. Somebody asking a question is usually missing context,
  not intelligence.
- **Say when you are unsure.** Confident wrongness is the most expensive thing in a
  codebase whose output people may risk money on.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sustained disruption, publishing private
information, or unwelcome sexual attention. None of it is interesting and all of it drives
away people who would otherwise have helped.

## Enforcement

Report through the contact page at https://stratify.aeon-labs.site/contact, or privately via **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository if it concerns conduct in a security report. Reports stay confidential.

The maintainers will respond in a way they judge proportionate — a private word, an edit,
a temporary block, or a permanent one. Maintainers who behave badly are not exempt.

## Scope

Applies in this repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.
